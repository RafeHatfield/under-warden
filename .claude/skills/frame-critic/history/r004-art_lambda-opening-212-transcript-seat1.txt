RANK: 2 > 1 > 4 > 3

BEST: 2
BEST_WHY: The lit flagstone floor in the centre of 2 (the area around the figure and the stela, roughly x 200 to 560, y 460 to 720) is the best-made surface in the deck. The flags are different widths within a single course, the joints are not a uniform one-pixel grid but open and close along their length, and the dark chipping is clustered at block corners rather than sprinkled evenly, so the floor reads as cut and worn stone rather than a stamped tile. The wall face band at y 420 to 455 has its timber rail broken by posts at irregular spacing with a small pinned splice at x 440, which is exactly the "repaired with driven pins and salvaged timber" fiction without announcing it. Of the three prop sets, 2's is the quietest: the three cheval-de-frise stakes at the bottom left sit in shadow at a value close to the wall, and the leaning plank in the top-right notch (x 505 to 560, y 390 to 460) is small and tucked into a wall corner where a plank would actually be left. The light falloff from the figure into the dark rock at top left is smooth, with the wall-top slab cracks still faintly legible in the dark instead of crushed to black.

WORST: 3
WORST_WHY: 3 is a flat, evenly lit tileset with no falloff from the figure at all, and every surface in it is visibly stamped. The grey wall tops (left column, x 0 to 55, and the bottom band, y 215 to 265) carry a perfectly uniform one-pixel grid with identical speckle in every cell. The teal moss blobs on the left wall at about (35, 60) and (25, 220) are the same shape pasted twice. The two crates at (60, 10) and (60, 50) are identical copies, as are the two barrels at (130, 200) and (175, 200). The pool at right is a repeating hex texture at full tile edges with hard square corners and no bank, shore, or wetness on the adjacent floor. The brown floor is a single noise texture with red speckle repeated across the whole room. The orc stands on a flat oval shadow puddle while nothing else in the frame casts one. It looks like the free asset pack, not a paid game.
